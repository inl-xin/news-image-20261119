Entry for the make.fun bounty `news-image-20261119` (sum up 19 November's
world news in one image), by @inl-xin.

A newspaper-front-page infographic in GTA-game flat / pixel style:
masthead "Thursday 19 November 2026", region bar, the-day-in-UTC strip,
3 hero panels (Trump, Xi & Putin meet in Shenzhen — first-ever trilateral;
ICJ rules Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya; Houthi missile
hits Riyadh's PIF Tower), 13 more drawn panels (GTA VI launches, Anthropic
debuts on Nasdaq, Denmark ratifies the Greenland pact, Kerch Bridge hit
again, Cyclone Akvan, Gaza, North Korea, Bangladesh, COP31, Venezuela deal,
Hormuz tankers, Senegal boat, red crabs), a world map, "ALSO ON THURSDAY
19 NOVEMBER" briefs and a red DATE CHECK box (the Van Gogh $190.4m sale was
18 Nov; Cyclone Akvan landfall is 20 Nov; no Shenzhen breakthrough).

22 stories · 7 regions · 16 drawn · one page.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `./entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`). The image is a
pre-built asset; `draw.py` is the build-time script that drew it with
PIL and is kept in the repo for transparency.
